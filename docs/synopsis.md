# Synopsis - Vattroller X

Built from a read of the extracted script (no English guide exists). Event file ranges in brackets; they are the
chapter list for the story pass (`tools/pairs.py`, `references/translation.md` §Review). Names: `glossary.md`.

Vattrolling is a VR sport: a Vattroller fights in body form with Arms (weapons) and in ball form (Roll) wrapped in
aura, with an Armalet (a creature partner) and Arm Sets. Being knocked out of the ring = "Dropped". The top league is
the Vattleague; its champion faces "Mars", the title holder, on Kaiten Island.

## Chapters

1. **Vattroller Jin comic** [e2478-e2519]. The in-game comic book, in arcs (Debut, Showdown, Training, Arena, Side
   Story, Death Roll). Jin Tsukahara, a brash boy with the phoenix Armalet Firebird and his pet Muchapy, takes on
   rivals Ryo and Shu (both students of Master Reion), Totta ("Totta-sama") and the Tsuchioni Gang (Katamari,
   Reina), trains, enters the Arena, and faces the Death Rollers. Side stories give Ryo, Shu and Totta their own
   episodes (Kanata's Mugendo VR shop and her brother Aoi; dojo master Inogashira).
2. **RPG mode: the club** [e2520-e2530]. The player (`∴`, "you") transfers from Mamaru Middle to Katsutobi Middle
   and joins Komachi's Vattrolling Club. Komachi is proud ("Komachi-sama"), runs a Japanese-sweets shop family, and
   has a crush on "Shu-sama"; her big brother Raiba trains under Master West of the Premium Gym. Takumi (student
   council president, ex-Premium Gym) and Ikazuchi (Kansai drawl, from Tamari Village, joins after a bet) make the
   club four: "Club Force". The Premium Gym twins Minami ("Ohohoho") and Hokuto ("little lady") are rivals.
3. **RPG mode: Vattleague** [e2531-e2549]. Mr. Globe (Globe Corp. president, sleepy "...mnya" old man) and the
   qualifier; Mars Hayate; Raiba and Master West; the player wins the Vattleague, earns the Mars Pass, fights Mars at
   Kaiten Colosseum, and the ending plays (e2549). Late reveal: Asama, Komachi and Raiba's father, was once the
   Vattleague No. 1 who never became Mars; Raiba wants to become Mars in his place.
4. **Post-game: Jin crossover** [e2550-e2562]. Jin and the comic cast meet the player; Todo's illegal Arms.
5. **Side events** [e2563-e2603]. Aura Mate theft, ghost prank, Shark Ball mishap, Secret Scroll, cat hostage
   (Kodama = Komachi's cat), part-time jobs, beach cans, Suzuka's bottle letters to Mars and the Suzuka Grand Prix,
   mochi job, Kodama rescue, chestnut "romance", movie audition, fire training, Corona's knitting (Armalet Angelan).
6. **Town and gyms** [e2603-e2711]. NPCs of Tamari Village (dialects, Fake Kansai Patrol, Ikazuchi's granny Washu),
   Dandahama beach (haiku man, trade chain, Kunigami's beach gym), Mangetsu, Globe Dept. Store and HQ, the Arena,
   Katsutobi and Mamaru Middle (Sakura-sensei the librarian), Vattleague reception, V Center link counters, V Machine
   tutorials and library rule books; e2711 is a debug copy of town scripts.
7. **System text** [ROM strings, credits]. Menus, items, Arms, Armalets, V Skills, VRX character profiles (incl.
   crossover guests), place names, credits.

## Relationships and reveals to keep

- Raiba is Komachi's big brother (she calls him "big brother"); Asama is their father.
- Kodama is a cat, not a person. ケイコ is always 稽古 "training", never a name.
- Mr. Globe = the sleepy old man; do not give his identity away before the script does.
- Readings of 勝飛 Katsutobi, 真丸 Mamaru, 田万里 Tamari, 弾打浜 Dandahama are guesses (no kana in the script).
